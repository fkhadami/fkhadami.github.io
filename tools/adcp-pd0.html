Quiver: arrows point the way the current flows (north up), each one a vector average over its time window, drawn so that East and North share one scale.

Current rose

Sv = C + 10 log((T+273.16)·R²) − 10 log(L) − 10 log(P) + 2αR + 10 log(10^(Kc(E−Er)/10) − 1), with R the slant range to the cell, α from Ainslie & McColm (1998) using the recorded temperature and salinity, and the last term reduced to Kc(E−Er) when noise correction is off (Deines 1999; Mullison 2017). Relative backscatter drops C, L, P and the temperature term. Kc, Er and C are instrument-specific: the defaults are typical Workhorse values, so calibrate against water samples before reading the numbers as concentrations. Averaging uses the same windows as the velocities, done in linear units.

Why cells were removed

A cell can fail several tests, so counts can add up to more than the total removed. “Missing in file” means the instrument already flagged it.
